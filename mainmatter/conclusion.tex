\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{chapter:conclusion}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Start the conclusion by repeating the objective of your research.
    \item Present an answer/conclusion to your objective.
    \item Provide support for this conclusion (Why is this the answer? Give arguments, answers to key questions.)
    \item Discuss your results in comparison to the results of other researchers.
    \item Describe the implications and limitations to this answer.
    \item Give suggestions for future research.
    \item The conclusion can be read independently.
    \item You end on a positive note.
\end{itemize}
